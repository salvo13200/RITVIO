Choose your download
How do I install on Android?

Download the official APK above and open it. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. Updates to the official app should be installed over it, without uninstalling, to keep your library.

Android release notes and checksums
Which Windows download should I choose?

The installer is the simplest everyday choice. The portable ZIP works after one extraction; open RITVIO.exe with its adjacent files in place. The self-extracting EXE unpacks each time and may start more slowly.

Windows packages are not Authenticode-signed, so SmartScreen may show a warning. Read the official release notes before continuing.

Are these stable releases?

The release channel is shown beside each download. Read the notes for the tested scope, update instructions and known limitations.

Older packages remain available in the release archive.

Does my library sync across devices?

Your library stays on the device where you create it. Windows and Android libraries are separate. You can exchange supported playlist files; hosted accounts and automatic cloud sync are not available.

Where do the music and lyrics come from?

Catalogue and playback depend on unofficial YouTube / YouTube Music resolvers. Lyrics come from LRCLIB when a match is available. External services can change, fail or limit requests; RITVIO is not affiliated with them.

The app’s GPLv3 licence does not grant rights to music, lyrics or artwork. See sources and attribution for details.

What does this page collect?

This page adds no advertising or analytics trackers. Checking releases and downloading files contact GitHub. Hosting providers may keep technical access logs. Platform recommendations are made locally in your browser.
