Regenerate it with `pnpm licenses:collect` after a locked installation and `pnpm setup:resolver`. Development tools are separately pinned in `pnpm-lock.yaml`, not all included in this runtime inventory. Missing notices cause the collector to fail.

Other working mockups, downloaded comparison APKs, private screenshots and design skill packages are excluded from the public source export.
